How It Works

1

Set Up Your Phone

Position your camera to the side. Chronicle needs to see your key joints -- hip and knee for lower body, shoulder and wrist for upper body.

2

Calibrate

Stand still for a moment. Chronicle auto-calibrates to your body proportions using your height, establishing baseline measurements.

3

Lift

Perform your exercise. Chronicle tracks your reps in real-time, measuring depth, velocity, and speed scores on every rep.

4

Review

Save your set and see fatigue drop-off, depth quality, and velocity trends. All your data is stored in your workout dashboard.
